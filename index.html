Privacy Policy for Bitezy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Bitezy ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information Bitezy accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and what choices you have.

By using Bitezy, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Access

Camera & Barcode Scanning

Bitezy requests access to your device's camera solely to scan and analyse food product barcodes.

Barcode scanning is performed using Google ML Kit. The camera is used for the scanning process and Bitezy does not intentionally record, save, or upload your camera photos or video feeds to our servers.

Camera access is only used when you actively use the barcode scanning feature.

Barcode Information

When you scan a barcode, Bitezy uses the barcode number to retrieve information about the corresponding food product.

The barcode itself is used to identify the product and retrieve nutritional information. Bitezy does not use scanned barcodes to personally identify you.

2. Food & Nutrition Data

Bitezy retrieves food product information from Open Food Facts, an open food database.

Depending on the product, this information may include:

  • Product name
  • Ingredients
  • Nutritional information
  • Nutri-Score
  • Traffic-light nutritional ratings
  • Product images and other publicly available product information

Retrieved product information may be cached and stored locally on your device using the Android Room database so that the app can provide a faster experience.

Bitezy does not operate the Open Food Facts database. Information obtained from Open Food Facts is subject to the applicable policies and terms of that service.

3. Local Data Storage

Bitezy stores certain app data locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Scanned food products
  • Food diary/history information
  • Your app preferences
  • Cached food and nutritional information

This information is stored locally and is not intentionally uploaded by Bitezy to a Bitezy-operated server.

You can delete individual items from your history or clear your entire scan history through the app.

Uninstalling Bitezy may also remove locally stored app data, depending on your device's Android settings and backup configuration.

4. Advertising

Bitezy is provided free of charge and is supported by advertising through Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, app interaction information, and other information required to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable laws.

Advertising may be personalised or non-personalised depending on applicable requirements and your choices.

Bitezy does not directly collect or store advertising information on its own servers. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google handles information in connection with advertising, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Third-Party Services

Bitezy uses third-party services to provide certain functionality.

Google ML Kit
Used for barcode scanning and on-device image analysis.

Google AdMob
Used to display advertisements and measure advertising performance.

Open Food Facts
Used to retrieve publicly available food product and nutritional information.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

Bitezy does not sell your personal information.

Bitezy does not intentionally share your personal information with third parties for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the app.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob and Open Food Facts may receive or process information as necessary to provide their respective services, as described above.

7. Your Choices and Data Control

You have control over information stored locally by Bitezy.

You can:

  • Delete individual food records from your history.
  • Clear your entire scan history.
  • Disable camera permission through your Android device settings.
  • Manage advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and available Google settings.

Because most Bitezy user data is stored locally on your device, Bitezy does not provide a remote account-based data deletion system for locally stored scan history.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Bitezy.

Your food diary, scan history, preferences, and cached food information are primarily stored locally on your device.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Bitezy is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Bitezy. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the details below.

10. Data Retention

Locally stored Bitezy data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to your device's operating system and backup settings.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is retained according to the respective third party's policies and applicable legal requirements.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Bitezy, third-party services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, we will update the "Effective Date" at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
