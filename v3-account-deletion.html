Delete your Bitezy account

Account and data deletion

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Bitezy and tap the account icon on Home.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm your identity using your password or Google.

When completed in the app, this deletes your Bitezy sign-in account and clears food records and preferences on this device, including the diary, scan history, favourites, saved meals, plans, shopping lists and ingredient preferences.

Request deletion without the app

Email vhstudios7@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Bitezy account, using the subject Bitezy account deletion request. Tell us whether you used Google or email/password sign-in. Do not send your password, payment details or medical information.

We will verify account ownership before processing your request and confirm the outcome by email. The request covers your Bitezy/Firebase Authentication account and its associated sign-in profile information. We cannot remotely erase the food diary stored on your phone; use Android Settings → Apps → Bitezy → Storage → Clear storage/data to remove those records.

Google Play subscriptions are separate

Deleting your Bitezy account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. To stop future renewals, open Google Play subscriptions and cancel Bitezy Premium. Deleting a Bitezy account does not delete your Google account.

Information retained by service providers

Google Play retains billing and transaction information under its own policies and legal obligations. Diagnostic and support records may be retained as needed for service operation, support and applicable legal requirements. Account deletion does not automatically erase information already processed by independent advertising or diagnostic providers; their privacy policies explain available controls and retention.
